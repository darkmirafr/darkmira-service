Does this sound familiar?

Many small businesses and SMEs run on a custom application built ten or fifteen years ago. It handles quotes, inventory, invoicing. It keeps the business running, but it has become hard to maintain.

And nobody really knows how it works any more, for one reason or another: the agency closed, the developer left, or they're still around but won't touch it.

The problem isn't finding someone to do the work. It's making a decision while keeping control of what happens next.

We analyse. You choose.

  1. We analyse

    The code, the environment and the problems you are facing. A short assignment, quoted and invoiced.

  2. We write down what the application really does

    The business rules, what's broken, what's risky, what can evolve, all written in plain language. This document is yours once the assignment is paid for.

  3. You choose where we start

    Based on a clear roadmap that a decision-maker can commit to.

What AI makes possible today is doing in a few days the analysis and development work that used to take us several weeks.

After the analysis, you choose what comes next.

  • Fix and maintain

    Get the application working properly again and keep it running.

  • Rebuild as is

    Rebuild what needs rebuilding, without changing what the application does.

  • Rethink the application

    Review with you what it should do. This is a separate project, priced separately and proposed in the roadmap.

What we guarantee

Nothing strategic or critical goes into production without human oversight.

We work under a best-efforts obligation, like any IT services company. You remain responsible for your application, just as you do for your accounts when working with your accountant.

You choose who approves, from the start.

It depends on who, in your company, can assess a technical change.

  • You have someone in-house who can assess a technical change.

    You approve

  • Someone in your company can follow along, without being a specialist.

    We advise, you approve

  • Nobody in your company can assess a technical change.

    We approve on your behalf
